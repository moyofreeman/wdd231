Site Name:

www.mabantuprimary.ac.zw

Site Purpose:

This website serves as an information hub for Mabantu Primary School. It has an application form for a prospective student. It provides learners, parents, prospective students and the general public with information about the school, including its location, demographics, curriculum, enrolment procedures, fee structure, academic calendar, school projects and employment opportunities.

Scenarios:

  • What are the school fees and other financial requirements?
  • Is there an online application portal?
  • What main and extracurricular activities are available?
  • Where is the school located in relation to Gwanda Town?
  • When does the school term begin and end?
  • Who is responsible for managing the school?
